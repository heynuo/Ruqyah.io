Why Zero Trackers, Zero Telemetry, and Zero Ads

Modern publishing has embraced an adversarial covenant with readers. Web pages now bundle megabytes of surveillance telemetry, fingerprinting scripts, algorithmic feed traps, and sponsored distractions. This environment is fundamentally incompatible with cognitive tranquility (Sakinah) and serious scholastic deliberation.

“To subject someone reading the word of God or studying a technical proof to tracking pixels is a breach of sacred hospitality and intellectual decency.”

Ruqyah adheres strictly to the following inviolable principles:

  • ✓
    Zero Third-Party CDNs or Dynamic Script Injections: All typography, audio players, and assets are hosted from pristine self-contained endpoints or strictly vetted archival repositories.
  • ✓
    Complete Privacy Guarantee: No Google Analytics, no Facebook Pixels, no cloud telemetry beacons, and no behavioral profiling. Your study sessions, bookmarks, and audio positions reside solely in your browser's private local storage.
  • ✓
    Offline Archival Resilience: Key articles and the 108 Master Verses scripture tracks can be cached transparently by your browser Service Worker for offline spiritual shelter during transit or remote retreats.

How This Archive is Engineered & Sourced

Static Edge Distributed • 100% Deterministic

This digital platform is constructed with the same architectural frugality as a bare-metal kernel module. We believe software longevity is achieved by removing dependencies, not accumulating them.

01

Software Architecture

  • › Semantic HTML5: Pure semantic tags for true screen-reader document outlines.
  • › Tailwind Tokens: Strict design token system without arbitrary runtime CSS-in-JS.
  • › Vanilla ES Modules: Zero heavy JavaScript frameworks (No React, Angular, or Vue).
  • › Browser Storage: LocalStorage for bookmarking and playback progress; no tracking DB.
Bundle footprint: 0 KB JS dependencies
02

Textual Scripture Canon

  • › King Fahd Complex: Verified Quranic Uthmani text conforming to the Madinah Mushaf standard.
  • › Tanzil Project: Verified digital text encoding and pause-mark verification algorithms.
  • › Saheeh International: Clear, orthodox English translation utilized for primary readings.
  • › Amiri Typography: High-legibility classical Naskh font for sacred Arabic orthography.
Char encoding: UTF-8 Strict
03

Recitation Pipeline

  • › EveryAyah CDN: Unmodified, verified ayah-by-ayah audio streams at 128 kbps.
  • › Primary Reciter: Sheikh Mishary Rashid Alafasy, validated for clear Tajweed pacing.
  • › Offline Buffering: Browser CacheStorage via ServiceWorker for low-bandwidth resilience.
  • › Autonomous Sync: Time scrubber synced to millisecond timestamps without server polling.
