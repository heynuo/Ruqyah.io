About This Compilation: Background, Methodology & Key Statistics

From Page 2 of Official PDF

This document presents a master compilation of 108 unique Quranic verse references extracted through the systematic analysis of 62 books spanning Islamic sciences, Ruqyah and healing traditions, Seerah (prophetic biography), Tafsir (Quranic exegesis), and Islamic history. Each entry has been deduplicated so that no verse reference appears more than once, providing a clean, authoritative reference covering 44 chapters (Surahs) of the Holy Quran.

The analysis was conducted using a multi-agent parallel processing approach, enabling simultaneous examination of numerous books to ensure comprehensive coverage. Verses are organized in canonical order — from Surah Al-Fatiha (Chapter 1) through to Surah An-Nas (Chapter 114) — and each entry records the Surah name, verse reference, thematic context, and the source book from which it was extracted.

menu_book Note on Source Books

Of the 62 books in the analyzed collection, approximately 30 are Western occult and non-Islamic texts containing zero Quranic content. The remaining 17–18 Islamic books yielded all 108 unique verse references documented here. Several Arabic-script manuscripts (including multiple copies of Shams al-Maarif) could not be text-extracted due to scanned/image formats.

The two primary contributing books are Understanding Ruqyah and Jinn in Islam (contributing the vast majority) and Ism-e-Azam (Ilmul Hisab) (contributing 4 specialized verses connected to Prophetic names of Allah: Yahya/AS 19:5, Musa/AS 20:13, Sulaiman/AS 38:35, Muhammad/SAW 43:29).
