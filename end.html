The End is but the Beginning

Every seeker journeys through a season of striving, every breath measures an appointed decree, and every soul returns unto the One Who initiated it. In Qur'anic wisdom, the “End” is not oblivion—it is the unveiling of truth, the arrival at eternal tranquility, and the ultimate homecoming to Allah.

menu_book SURAH AN-NAJM (53:42) JUZ 27

وَأَنَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ الْمُنتَهَىٰ

“And that to your Lord is the final end and ultimate destination.”

Husn al-Khātimah

حُسْنُ الخَاتِمَةِ

The blessed conclusion. In Islamic tradition, actions are judged by their conclusions (*al-a'mālu bi-khawātīmihā*). Sincerity at the finish line sanctifies the entire journey of life.

Saheeh al-Bukhari 6493

SURAH AL-FAJR (89:27-30)

The Call to the Serene Soul

يَا أَيَّتُهَا النَّفْسُ الْمُطْمَئِنَّةُ ۝ ارْجِعِي إِلَىٰ رَبِّكِ رَاضِيَةً مَّرْضِيَّةً ۝ فَادْخُلِي فِي عِبَادِي ۝ وَادْخُلِي جَنَّتِي ۝

“O tranquil soul! Return to your Lord, well-pleased and pleasing [to Him], and enter among My righteous servants, and enter My Paradise.”

Yā ayyatuhan-nafsul-muṭma'innah • Irji'ī ilā Rabbiki rāḍiyatan marḍiyyah • Fadkhulī fī 'ibādī • Wadkhulī jannatī.
